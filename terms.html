Users must meet the minimum age, country and account requirements displayed at launch and provide accurate information. One person may not operate multiple accounts to obtain duplicate benefits.

3. Points

Points are promotional virtual units. They are not legal tender, do not constitute a bank deposit and have no value outside Prize Boss. Availability, thresholds, limits and expiry rules must be shown before they apply.

4. Activities and third parties

Some activities may be supplied and verified by third-party offer providers. Each activity may have separate eligibility and completion conditions. Starting an activity does not guarantee a reward; the required event must be validly confirmed.

5. Rewards

Reward types, availability, minimum thresholds, processing times and any applicable checks will be displayed before a request is confirmed. Production redemptions are not yet enabled.

6. Prohibited conduct

Automation, false information, device manipulation, duplicate accounts, interference with tracking, fraudulent completions and attempts to exploit the service are prohibited. Suspicious rewards may be held while reviewed.

7. Suspension and closure

Access may be limited when reasonably necessary for security, compliance, investigation or violation of these terms. Users will be able to request account deletion subject to legal retention requirements.

8. Changes and availability

The service, activities and rewards may change. Material changes to final production terms will be communicated as required by law.

The operator’s complete registered details, governing-law provisions and support contact will be inserted after business verification and before the public launch.
