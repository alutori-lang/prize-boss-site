Terms of Service

Draft for pre-launch review · Updated 28 October 2026

Pre-launch notice: these terms describe the intended service and require final legal and commercial review before public release.

1. Service

Prize Boss is intended to let eligible users discover activities, receive virtual points after confirmed completion and request rewards that are available in their region and account.

2. Eligibility

You can play and collect points at any age allowed by Google Play for this app, but you must be at least 18 years old to request a cash payout. One person may hold only one account; duplicate accounts are not paid.

3. Points

Points are promotional virtual units. They are not legal tender and are not a bank deposit. There are two kinds: partner points, earned by completing paid surveys (CPX Research), partner offers (AdGem) or rewarded videos (Google AdMob), each confirmed by the partner; and app points, from games, missions and the welcome bonus. Only partner points can be cashed out. App points have no cash value and can be used only inside Prize Boss.

4. Activities and third parties

Some activities may be supplied and verified by third-party offer providers. Each activity may have separate eligibility and completion conditions. Starting an activity does not guarantee a reward; the required event must be validly confirmed.

5. Rewards and payouts

Partner points can be cashed out with PayPal at the rate shown in the app (currently 2,000 points = 1 EUR), in whole euros, from a minimum of 5 EUR up to 50 EUR per request, with one open request at a time. Partner points become withdrawable a few days after they are earned (currently 3 days), because partners may cancel a completion they later find invalid; cancelled completions are removed from the balance. When you send a request the points leave your balance immediately. We review every request and pay it to the PayPal address you give, normally within 7 working days. A rejected request returns the points to your balance. We do not charge you any fee; PayPal may apply its own conditions to your PayPal account. You are responsible for entering the correct PayPal address and for any taxes that apply to you.

6. Prohibited conduct

Automation, emulators or bots, false information in surveys, device manipulation, VPNs used to hide your country, duplicate accounts, interference with tracking, fraudulent completions and attempts to exploit the service are prohibited. We may hold or reject a payout and remove points obtained this way.

7. Suspension and closure

Access may be limited when reasonably necessary for security, compliance, investigation or violation of these terms. Users will be able to request account deletion subject to legal retention requirements.

8. Changes and availability

The service, activities and rewards may change. Material changes to final production terms will be communicated as required by law.

9. Operator and contact

The operator’s complete registered details, governing-law provisions and support contact will be inserted after business verification and before the public launch.
