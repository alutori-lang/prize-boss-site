Prize Boss is currently in development. Real offers and reward redemptions are not yet publicly available.

Before contacting us

  • Never send your password, verification code or complete payment credentials.
  • For an activity issue, keep the activity name, date, device and any confirmation reference.
  • For privacy requests, clearly state whether you need access, correction or deletion.
