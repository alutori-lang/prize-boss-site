Privacy Policy

Updated 17 October 2026

Prize Boss ("the app") is published by DreamcodeStudio. This policy explains what the app does with information in its current version.

1. What the app collects

The current version of Prize Boss does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal data. It does not send your points, games or history to any server.

  • Points, missions and activity history are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera are accessed.
  • No advertising or analytics SDK is included in this version.

2. Links to other services

Some screens may open web pages (for example this policy or the support page) in your browser. Those pages are governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Rewards

This version is a preview. Rewards cannot be redeemed and no money, prize or gift card is sent. Points are virtual and have no cash value. If a future version allows redeeming rewards, this policy will be updated before that feature is available, and you will be told what information is needed.

4. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your phone's settings, deletes all points and history. Nothing is kept elsewhere.

5. Children

Prize Boss is not directed at children under 13.
