Privacy Policy

Draft for pre-launch review · Updated 15 October 2026

Important: Prize Boss is in development. This policy must be completed with the legal company identity and production contact details before public release.

1. Data controller

The service will be operated by the legal business identified in the app-store listing and publisher account. The final registered name, address and privacy contact will be published here before user registration is enabled.

2. Data we expect to process

  • Account identifiers and contact information supplied by the user.
  • Device, app, language and technical diagnostic information.
  • Activity, offer, points, reward and transaction records.
  • Security signals used to prevent fraud, abuse and duplicate rewards.
  • Support messages and information needed to resolve requests.

3. Purposes

Data may be used to provide accounts and activities, verify eligible completions, maintain points and reward records, prevent abuse, answer support requests, comply with legal obligations and improve reliability.

4. Legal bases

Depending on the processing, the legal basis may be performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests in service security and fraud prevention, or consent where required.

5. Partners and international transfers

Prize Boss may use hosting, analytics, customer-support, fraud-prevention, offer and payment providers. The final production policy will name relevant categories and provide information about safeguards for transfers outside the European Economic Area.

6. Retention and security

Information will be retained only as long as necessary for the stated purposes, legal requirements, dispute resolution and fraud prevention. Appropriate technical and organisational safeguards will be applied.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, and may withdraw consent. Users may also complain to their competent data-protection authority.

8. Children

The production service will state and enforce its applicable minimum age before launch. Prize Boss is not currently accepting public user registrations through this website.

9. Contact

The verified privacy email and legal postal address will be added before public release. For pre-launch enquiries, use the support page.
