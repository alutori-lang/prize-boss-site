Privacy Policy

Updated 28 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

Prize Boss is run by DreamcodeStudio, which is the data controller for the processing described here. Privacy contact: salsostaff20@gmail.com.

2. Your anonymous account

The first time you open the app, Prize Boss creates an anonymous account for your phone. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number. The account is a random identifier with a sign-in token, which the app keeps on your phone so your points stay linked to you.

3. Data we process

  • Account data: the random account identifier, when the account was created and the sign-in tokens.
  • Points records: every change to your points balance, with its reason (for example a finished game, a claimed mission or a completed partner offer) and the time it happened.
  • Partner offer completions: if partner offers are available in the app and you use them, our offer partner AdGem tells our server which offer you completed and how many points it is worth, using your anonymous account identifier. AdGem processes the data you give it under its own privacy policy.
  • Paid surveys: if paid surveys are available in the app and you open them, the survey partner CPX Research (Make Opinion GmbH) receives your anonymous account identifier to show you surveys, and tells our server which survey you completed (or later cancelled) and how many points it is worth. Your survey answers, and any details you choose to give in a survey, go to CPX Research and its survey providers under their own privacy policy, not to us.
  • Rewarded videos (ads): if videos are available in the app and you choose to watch one, Google AdMob shows the video and processes your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, interactions with the ad and diagnostic data, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, under Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland Google's consent form asks for your choice first, and you can change it any time in Profile > Ads privacy. When you finish a video, Google tells our server your anonymous account identifier so we can add the points.
  • Install identifier: a random ID the app creates on your phone. Our server stores only a one-way hash of it, to give the welcome points once per installation and to prevent abuse.
  • Security and technical data: server logs, including the IP address of requests, used to keep the service running and to prevent cheating, fraud and duplicate rewards.
  • On your phone: the app stores the sign-in token, a copy of your last balance for offline display, and your game progress.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We use this data to run your account and keep your points balance (performance of our contract with you), to verify that games, missions and offers really took place and to stop abuse (our legitimate interest in a fair and secure service), and to meet legal obligations. Personalised ads in rewarded videos are shown only with your consent where the law requires it; otherwise Google shows non-personalised ads.

5. Where the data is stored

Your account and points records are stored on servers in the European Union (Ireland), operated for us by our hosting provider Supabase acting as a data processor. We do not sell your data.

6. Rewards

If you request a payout, we process the PayPal email address you enter, the amount and the date of the request, to pay you and to prevent fraud (performance of our contract with you and legal obligations). The address is used only to send the payment through PayPal, which acts under its own terms and privacy policy. Paid and rejected requests are kept for accounting and anti-fraud purposes; if you delete your account, the PayPal address is erased and only the amount and dates remain.

7. Retention

Points records are kept for as long as your account exists and are deleted with it. When you delete your account, partner offer completion records are kept only in anonymised form (no account ID) for fraud prevention and accounting, and the hashed install identifier is kept so the welcome points cannot be claimed again. Uninstalling the app removes the sign-in token from your phone; the anonymous account can then no longer be used from that phone.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and for data portability. You can delete your account and all its data yourself in the app under Profile → Delete my account and data (see Delete account). For any other request, write to salsostaff20@gmail.com with the account ID shown in Profile. You also have the right to complain to your data-protection authority.

9. Children

Prize Boss is not directed at children. Partner offers and rewards are only for users who meet the minimum age shown in the app-store listing.

10. Contact

DreamcodeStudio — salsostaff20@gmail.com. Changes to this policy are published on this page.
