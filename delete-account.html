Account controls

Delete your account

Public accounts are not yet enabled because Prize Boss is in pre-launch development.

When the service launches

Users will be able to start account deletion from inside the app and through a verified support channel published on this page.

What will be deleted

The account profile and personal information no longer required to provide the service will be deleted or anonymised. Some transaction, security or compliance records may be retained for the period required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent fraud and resolve disputes.

Before deletion

Pending support cases, reward reviews and available points may be affected. The app will show the consequences and request confirmation before deletion.

The production request form and verified support email will be added before account registration is opened.
