Account controls

Delete your account

You can delete your Prize Boss account and all its data at any time, directly in the app. No email or password is needed.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Prize Boss.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data.
  4. Read the summary and tap Delete permanently.

Deletion happens right away. The app then starts again with a new, empty anonymous account.

Delete by email

If you can no longer open the app, email salsostaff20@gmail.com with the subject “Prize Boss account deletion” and your account ID. The ID is shown in the app under Profile → Account ID (the copy button copies the full ID). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous account and its sign-in tokens.
  • Your points balance and complete points history (games, missions, partner offers).
  • The partner offer (AdGem) completion records linked to your account.

Nothing is kept on our server for a deleted account. Server logs used for security are deleted automatically by our hosting provider after a short period. Uninstalling the app also removes the data stored on your phone, but it does not delete the account on our server: use one of the options above for that.

Important: deletion cannot be undone. Points cannot be recovered or moved to a new account.
